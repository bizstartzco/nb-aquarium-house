Holiday & Away Care

Someone who knows what a sick fish looks like, rather than a neighbour with a tub of flake.

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Holiday & Away Care

How it works

The classic holiday disaster is not starvation. Most adult fish are fine for a week without food. It is a well-meaning helper tipping in a fortnight of flake on day two, the surplus rotting, and ammonia climbing while nobody is watching.

We feed to a written amount, check the equipment, and send a photograph so you can see the tank rather than take our word for it.

Included

  • Measured feeding, so nothing is overfed into an ammonia spike
  • Equipment check: heater, filter flow, CO2, timers
  • Test and partial water change on longer trips
  • Photographs sent after every visit
