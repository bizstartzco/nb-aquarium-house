How it works

From our tanks to yours

Why buying a fish from us takes a little longer than buying one from a chain shop.

01 / 04

STEP 01

Fourteen days in quarantine

Every fish lands in our own isolated system first. It is watched, fed and treated if it needs to be, and only reaches the sales floor once it is eating and behaving normally.

STEP 02

We test your water

Bring a sample from home. If your tap water is wrong for the species you want, we would rather tell you at the counter than sell you the fish twice.

STEP 03

Choose with someone who keeps them

The people behind the counter keep planted tanks and ponds themselves. Ask about tank size, lighting or whether two species will get along, and you will get a straight answer.

STEP 04

Carried home, or driven there

Fish are bagged with oxygen and packed for the journey. Inside the Kathmandu Valley we will deliver living stock ourselves; it never goes by courier.
