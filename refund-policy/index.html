Policy

Returns and refunds

Unused dry goods come back within 30 days. Livestock cannot, and here is exactly why.

Dry goods

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of purchase with proof of purchase, for exchange or refund.

Opened test kits, treatments, foods, fertilisers and substrate cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons unless they are faulty.

Faulty equipment

Heaters, filters, pumps and lighting carry the manufacturer warranty. Bring the item and the receipt and we will handle the claim rather than sending you to the importer.

Tell us what the tank was doing when it failed. A heater that cooked a tank and a heater that simply stopped are different faults with different causes.

Livestock

Livestock cannot be returned for a change of mind. An animal that has been in another system cannot go back into ours without putting everything else at risk.

If an animal is unwell, contact us within 24 hours with a photograph and your water readings. See the delivery and live arrival policy for how we handle it.

Client action required. Have this checked against Nepali consumer law before the site goes live.

Bring us your water.

About 200 ml in a clean container, taken before a water change. We test it at the counter for free, whether or not you buy anything.
